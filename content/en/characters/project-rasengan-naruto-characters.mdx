## Team 7 and the Characters You Meet First

Most players start with Team 7, and for good reason. These four characters teach the fundamentals: spacing, chakra management, and when to commit to a finisher.

| Character | Role | Signature Jutsu | Playstyle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Naruto Uzumaki | Protagonist / all-rounder | Rasengan, Shadow Clone Jutsu | Aggressive rushdown with burst windows |
| Sasuke Uchiha | Rival / duelist | Chidori, Fire Style | Precision counters and punish tools |
| Sakura Haruno | Support / bruiser | Chakra-Enhanced Strength | Sustain, healing, heavy single hits |
| Kakashi Hatake | Mentor / tactician | Raikiri, Kamui | Versatile, rewards adaptation |

Naruto is the natural first pick because his kit mirrors the project's theme. His Shadow Clones create openings, and the Rasengan closes them. Sasuke is the opposite lesson: patience. Players who rush with him tend to lose, while players who bait and punish tend to climb.

Sakura often gets overlooked early and then becomes essential in longer fights, where her healing and durability outlast burst-oriented opponents. Kakashi is the "learn the game" character — his flexibility forces you to understand every matchup instead of memorizing one combo.

## Playstyle Tips and Roster Progression

Once you have a feel for the roster, progression becomes about efficiency. Here is what experienced players tend to recommend.

- **Learn one Rasengan variant deeply before chasing another.** Muscle memory beats variety early on.
- **Pair a burst character with a sustain character.** Naruto plus Sakura covers more matchups than two rushdown picks.
- **Practice counters, not combos.** Most losses against Akatsuki come from pressing buttons at the wrong time.
- **Revisit earlier characters after unlocking legends.** The fundamentals you learn with Team 7 transfer directly to Kage-tier play.
- **Track your own matchups.** A short written log beats guessing why a fight felt bad.

| Goal | Recommended Focus | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn fundamentals | Naruto, Kakashi | Flexible kits that punish bad habits |
| Learn defense | Sakura, Gaara | Survive long enough to read patterns |
| Learn precision | Sasuke, Minato | Rewards timing over aggression |
| Learn control | Itachi, Pain | Teaches space and resource denial |

Community reports suggest the roster expands over time, so treat any current tier list as temporary. The characters that age well are almost always the ones with clear strengths and honest weaknesses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Project Rasengan an official Naruto game?**
No. It is a fan-made project, and it is not affiliated with the official franchise. If you enjoy the source material, supporting the official releases through the [official Naruto portal](https://naruto-official.com/en) is the best way to keep the series thriving.

**Which Project Rasengan Naruto characters should a beginner main first?**
Start with Naruto Uzumaki. His Shadow Clone setup and Rasengan finisher teach the project's core loop, and his weaknesses are easy to understand. Kakashi Hatake is the strongest second pick for players who want to learn matchups rather than combos.

**Do the Project Rasengan Naruto characters include Boruto-era fighters?**
Player experience varies here. Some builds include Boruto-era additions such as Vanishing Rasengan users, while others stay strictly within the Naruto and Naruto Shippuden timeline. Check the current roster in your build rather than assuming.

**How long does it take to unlock legendary characters?**
It depends entirely on the build and the progression system in place. Rather than grinding blindly, focus on completing objectives that reward roster unlocks, and prioritize the characters whose jutsu you actually want to learn.

The appeal of Project Rasengan is that it takes one technique and asks how an entire cast would answer it.
